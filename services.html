Our Services

Comprehensive Workforce Solutions — From Labour Contracting to Government Apprenticeship Schemes

What We Offer

End-to-End Workforce Services

Kunal Group is Pune's trusted partner for industrial workforce solutions. Whether you need workers on-site today, want to leverage government apprenticeship schemes to reduce costs, or require a fully-managed contract staffing model — we have the expertise, network, and infrastructure to deliver.

Labour Contracting

We supply skilled, semi-skilled, and unskilled workers to manufacturing plants, warehouses, and project sites across Pune. All compliance — ESIC, PF, attendance, payroll — is fully managed by Kunal Group. You direct the work; we handle everything else.

NAPS

The National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme enables your company to engage ITI trade apprentices with 25% stipend reimbursement (up to ₹1,500/month) from the Government of India. We manage end-to-end portal operations on apprenticeshipindia.org, handling everything from registration to exit formalities.

25% Stipend Reimbursed ITI & Non-ITI Portal Management
Learn More

NATS

The National Apprenticeship Training Scheme, administered through the BOAT portal under the Ministry of Education, connects engineering graduates (BE/BTech) and diploma holders with hands-on industrial training. With over 2,500 active NATS trainees currently managed by Kunal Group, we are among Pune's leading NATS facilitators.

BE/BTech Graduates Diploma Holders 2,500+ Active Trainees

Staffing Contract
