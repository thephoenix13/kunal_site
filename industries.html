Industries We Serve

Sector-Specific Workforce Expertise Across Pune's Industrial Corridors

Sector Expertise

Two Decades of On-Ground Industry Knowledge

Kunal Group has been operating in Pune's industrial belt for over 20 years. Our understanding of sector-specific workforce requirements — the right skills, the right certifications, the right physical profile — means we do not supply generic workers. We supply workers who are ready to be productive in your specific environment from day one. Below are the six primary sectors we serve.

Automobile & Allied

Pune is India's automobile capital, and Kunal Group has been supplying workers to OEMs and Tier 1/2 suppliers in Chakan, Talegaon, and Khed SEZ for over two decades. We understand the precision, quality consciousness, and production pace that auto sector clients demand.

Typical Roles: Assembly Operators, MIG/TIG Welders, QC Inspectors, Material Handlers, Forklift Operators, Press Shop Workers, Paint Shop Helpers

Key Locations: Chakan MIDC, Talegaon MIDC, Khed SEZ, Ranjangaon

Engineering & Projects

From heavy fabrication yards to precision machine shops, Kunal Group supplies skilled and semi-skilled workers to engineering establishments across Pune's MIDC corridors. Our worker pool includes tradesmen with ITI and NCVT certifications in relevant trades.

Typical Roles: Fitters, Machinists, Electricians, Fabricators, Riggers, Structural Welders, NDT Technicians, Site Supervisors

Key Locations: Bhosari MIDC, Chakan MIDC, Pimpri-Chinchwad, Project Sites

Logistics & Warehousing

The rapid growth of e-commerce and organised retail distribution in Pune has created significant demand for warehouse and logistics workers. Kunal Group provides dependable, scalable workers for distribution centres and fulfilment operations — including seasonal scale-up during peak demand periods.

Typical Roles: Material Handlers, Loaders, Pickers, Packers, Forklift Operators, Inventory Staff, Security Personnel

Key Locations: Pune Logistics Corridors, Ranjangaon, Chakan, Sanaswadi

Construction

Pune's ongoing infrastructure development — residential, commercial, and industrial — requires large numbers of skilled and unskilled construction workers. Kunal Group supplies verified workers with experience in specific construction trades, reducing the risk of engaging unverified daily labour.

Typical Roles: General Labour, Masons, Bar Benders, Shuttering Carpenters, Tile Workers, Plasterers, Site Supervisors

Key Locations: Pune City, Pimpri-Chinchwad, PCMC areas, Hinjewadi, Wakad

Casting & Forging

Pune's casting and forging cluster — particularly around Chakan, Ranjangaon, and Bhosari — supplies critical components to the auto sector. Foundry and forging operations require workers familiar with high-temperature environments, heavy material handling, and specific process disciplines.

Typical Roles: Foundry Helpers, Die Casting Operators, Heat Treatment Workers, Fettling Operators, Shot Blasting Operators, QC Inspectors

Key Locations: Chakan, Ranjangaon, Bhosari MIDC

Rubber & Plastic

The polymer manufacturing sector in Pune — centred around Chakan and Sanaswadi — serves the automotive, packaging, and consumer goods industries. Workers in this sector require familiarity with moulding processes, production line disciplines, and basic quality control procedures.

Typical Roles: Injection Moulding Operators, Extrusion Line Workers, Finishing Helpers, Trimming Operators, QC Staff, Material Handlers

Key Locations: Chakan, Sanaswadi, Ranjangaon
