About Kunal Group

Two decades of building India's industrial workforce — one client relationship at a time.

Our Story

Where We Come From

Kunal Group was founded with a simple conviction: that factories and manufacturing units deserve a workforce partner they can truly rely on — one that shows up, follows through, and never compromises on compliance or quality.

Starting from Chakan — the heart of Pune's automobile manufacturing belt — we built our reputation one client at a time. Over 20 years, we have grown from a regional labour contractor to a full-service manpower solutions company serving 160+ clients including multinational corporations, Tier 1 auto suppliers, and large-scale project contractors.

Today we operate across Chakan, Sanaswadi, Ranjangaon, Bhosari, and Khed SEZ, and have expanded our services to a PAN India level. With 15,000+ workers currently under contract and 2,500+ active NAPS & NATS trainees, we have the depth and reach to match workforce needs at any scale.

Compliance

End-to-End Legal & Administrative Compliance

One of the biggest challenges of contract labour is managing the paperwork, statutory filings, and renewals. Kunal Group takes full responsibility so our clients never have to worry about a compliance notice.

What We Manage
  • PF (Provident Fund) — monthly challan generation and filing
  • ESIC — contribution management and challan filing
  • PT (Professional Tax) — state-wise compliance
  • WCI Policy (Workmen's Compensation Insurance) — maintenance & renewal
  • Labour Licence — procurement and renewal as per state rules
  • GST — compliance and documentation
  • All contract records maintained in fully computerised form
