Staffing Contract

Flexible, Scalable, Fully-Managed Workforce — Exactly When You Need It

Service Overview

The Employer of Record Model — Zero Headcount Risk

Under Kunal Group's Staffing Contract service, we function as the employer of record for your contract workforce. From recruitment through to exit, every employment obligation — payroll, PF, ESIC, minimum wages compliance, performance monitoring, and grievance management — sits entirely with Kunal Group. You issue the work instructions; we handle everything else.

This model is particularly valuable for companies facing seasonal demand peaks, project-based production bursts, or the need to test a workforce category before adding permanent headcount. There is no risk of the industrial dispute liability or retrenchment obligations that accompany direct permanent employment, because the employment relationship is legally with Kunal Group, not your company.

Your management team directs the contract workforce on-site — the workers follow your processes, your quality standards, your shift schedules. But every HR, payroll, and compliance function is outsourced to us, freeing your operations and finance teams to focus entirely on business outcomes.

Engagement Models
Fixed-Term Contract Defined headcount for a fixed period. Ideal for project execution, new line ramp-ups, and time-bound production orders.
Rolling Contract Ongoing engagement with quarterly headcount reviews. Suitable for stable operations with predictable workforce levels.
Seasonal Staffing Rapid scale-up and scale-down aligned to your production calendar. We maintain a ready pool for fast mobilisation during peak periods.

Scope of Service

What We Manage End-to-End

Every function in the contract workforce lifecycle is handled by Kunal Group. Your company receives a consolidated monthly invoice — no operational complexity on your side.

Function What Kunal Group Does
Recruitment Sourcing, screening, skills testing, and shortlisting of candidates matching your specification — roles, experience level, shift compatibility, and physical requirements.
Onboarding Document collection, PF/ESIC registration, ID verification, offer letters, joining formalities, and site induction coordination — all completed before Day 1 on site.
Training Basic safety induction, site-specific orientation, and coordination with your team leads for skill-specific on-the-job training.
Payroll Monthly salary computation, statutory deductions, bank transfers, salary slip generation, and full payroll register maintenance.
Statutory Compliance PF, ESIC, Minimum Wages Act, Bonus Act, Contract Labour (R&A) Act, and all other applicable labour law compliance — filings, challans, and inspection-readiness.
Performance Monitoring Attendance tracking, productivity reporting, and escalation management for absenteeism or performance issues — with immediate replacement provision where needed.
Grievance Management All worker grievances, HR queries, and industrial relations matters are managed by Kunal Group's HR team — your management is not directly involved.
Scaling Headcount adjustments — increases or reductions — processed efficiently as your production plan changes, with minimum notice periods as agreed in the contract.
Why It Works

The Business Case for Contract Staffing

Contract staffing with Kunal Group is not just an HR solution — it is a strategic business tool that gives your organisation measurable operational and financial advantages.

No Permanent Headcount Risk
Contract workers are on Kunal Group's payroll, not yours. Your company avoids the long-term obligations of permanent employment — retrenchment liability, gratuity accrual, and industrial dispute exposure are eliminated.
Legal Liability with Kunal Group
All employment liability — compliance, labour law, PF/ESIC notices, worker claims — rests with Kunal Group. Your company is protected from direct employer obligations for the contract workforce.
Your Team Focuses on Output
With Kunal Group managing all HR, payroll, and compliance functions, your plant HR and finance teams are freed from administrative burden and can focus entirely on production targets and operational efficiency.
Dedicated Account Manager
Every client is assigned a dedicated account manager who is your single point of contact for manpower requests, escalations, compliance reports, and billing. You always know who to call.
Our Process

From Requirement to Running Operation

A clear, five-step process gets your contract workforce operational with no ambiguity and no delays.

01
Define Requirement
We meet with your HR and operations team to document the number of workers, skill categories, shift patterns, site location, duration, engagement model, and any specific compliance or certification requirements.
02
Recruitment & Screening
We source candidates from our active pool and external networks, conduct skills assessments and background checks, and present a shortlist matching your specification for any approvals you wish to make.
03
Onboarding & Training
Selected candidates complete all joining formalities, statutory registrations, and safety inductions. We coordinate with your supervisors to ensure workers understand your site procedures before deployment.
04
Deployment
Workers are deployed to your facility on the agreed start date. Our operations team is available on Day 1 to ensure a smooth handover and address any immediate site-level concerns.
05
Ongoing Management & Reporting
Monthly payroll, compliance filings, attendance reports, and performance summaries are provided to your team. Your dedicated account manager conducts regular review meetings and manages all day-to-day workforce operations.
