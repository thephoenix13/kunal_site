NAPS — National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme

Reduce Your Manpower Costs While Building a Skilled Workforce Pipeline

What is NAPS

A Government Initiative That Pays You to Train

The National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme (NAPS) is a flagship initiative of the Government of India under the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. It is designed to encourage establishments to engage apprentices by sharing the cost of stipend payment. Under NAPS, the government reimburses 25% of the prescribed stipend amount, up to a maximum of ₹1,500 per apprentice per month, directly to the employer.

All NAPS activities — from employer registration to apprenticeship contract filing and reimbursement claims — are managed through the National Apprenticeship Portal at apprenticeshipindia.org. The scheme is governed by the Apprentices Act, 1961 and covers both ITI trade apprentices (Designated Trades) and non-ITI apprentices in Optional Trades.

For manufacturing companies in Pune, NAPS offers a direct and significant reduction in semi-skilled workforce costs — particularly in assembly, machine operation, and support functions. Benefits

Why Enrol Under NAPS

NAPS is one of the most under-utilised cost-reduction tools available to Indian manufacturers. Here is what your company gains.

Government Subsidy — 25%
The Government of India reimburses 25% of the stipend you pay, up to ₹1,500/month per apprentice. For a company with 100 apprentices, this translates to up to ₹1.8 lakh in annual savings.
Trained Workforce Pipeline
Apprentices trained within your facility learn your specific processes, quality standards, and equipment. The best performers become your permanent workforce without external recruitment costs.
Reduced Manpower Cost
Apprentice stipends are significantly lower than market wages for equivalent semi-skilled roles. Combined with the government reimbursement, NAPS provides the most cost-effective route to a steady semi-skilled workforce.
Compliance with Apprentices Act
Establishments above a certain workforce size are required by law to engage apprentices. NAPS provides a fully compliant, structured framework — avoiding penalties and ensuring you meet your statutory obligations.

Eligibility

Who Can Participate in NAPS

Company Eligibility

  • Any establishment with 4 or more workers (including contractual) is eligible
  • Must be registered under the Apprentices Act or willing to register
  • Should have basic training infrastructure (workstations, supervisors)
  • Manufacturing, services, construction, logistics — all sectors eligible
  • Both MSME and large corporates can participate
  • Company must have a valid GST/TAN/CIN for portal registration

Apprentice Eligibility

  • Age between 14 and 21 years (relaxations for SC/ST/OBC/PwD)
  • Passed Class 8 (minimum) or ITI certificate for designated trades
  • ITI pass-outs for Designated Trade apprenticeships
  • Class 5 pass minimum for certain Optional Trades
  • Must not be in full-time education during apprenticeship
  • Indian national; valid Aadhaar and bank account required
