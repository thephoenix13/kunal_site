What We Do

Zero-to-One Manpower Support

Whether you need daily-wage workers, trained apprentices, or a full staffing contract — we manage the entire workforce lifecycle for you.

Labour Contracting

We supply skilled, semi-skilled, and unskilled workers for continuous and project-based operations. All HR, payroll, attendance, and statutory compliance is managed by us — so you stay focused on production.

Skilled Workers Semi-skilled Full Compliance Immediate Deployment

NAPS — National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme

A Government of India initiative where companies receive 25% stipend reimbursement per apprentice. We handle the complete NAPS process — portal registration, enrolment, contract management, and government claims.

25% Govt Subsidy End-to-End Managed ITI & Non-ITI
Learn More

NATS — National Apprenticeship Training Scheme

For engineering graduates and diploma holders. Managed through the BOAT portal under the Ministry of Education. We handle trainee sourcing, documentation, compliance, and government reimbursement.

Engineering Graduates Diploma Holders BOAT Portal 2,500+ Active
Learn More

Staffing Contract

We act as employer of record for your contract workforce. Recruitment, onboarding, training, payroll, and compliance — all managed by us. Scale headcount up or down based on your production requirements.
