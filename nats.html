NATS — National Apprenticeship Training Scheme

Engage Engineering Graduates and Diploma Holders Through India's Premier Technical Apprenticeship Programme

What is NATS

Technical Talent at a Fraction of Market Cost

The National Apprenticeship Training Scheme (NATS) is administered by the Board of Apprenticeship and Training (BOAT) under the Ministry of Education, Government of India. It is specifically designed for technical degree and diploma holders — BE/BTech graduates, diploma holders in engineering, and vocationally trained students — who require industry exposure to consolidate their academic knowledge.

NATS traineeships typically run for 6 to 12 months. During this period, the government covers a portion of the stipend cost, making NATS trainees one of the most cost-effective sources of junior technical talent available to Indian manufacturers and engineering companies. Trainees work directly under your designated Training Officers, contributing productive work while developing job-ready skills.

Eligibility

Who Can Participate in NATS

Company Eligibility

  • Registered company/establishment in India (any sector)
  • Must have adequate training capacity and workstations for trainees
  • Designated Training Officers (DTOs) must be appointed
  • Must register on the BOAT portal (nats.education.gov.in)
  • Both MSMEs and large corporates are eligible
  • Manufacturing, R&D, services, and IT establishments can participate

Trainee Eligibility

  • BE/BTech degree holders in engineering disciplines
  • 3-year diploma holders from polytechnics
  • Vocational certificate holders (MES/NCVT)
  • Must not have completed a prior NATS/NAPS apprenticeship
  • Trainee duration: 6–12 months depending on qualification
  • Valid Aadhaar, bank account, and educational certificates required
