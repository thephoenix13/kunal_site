Labour Contracting

Reliable, Vetted, and Immediately Available Workforce for Your Operations

Service Overview

Your Operations, Our Workforce

Kunal Group supplies skilled, semi-skilled, and unskilled contract workers to manufacturing plants, fabrication yards, logistics centres, and project sites across Pune and the surrounding MIDC corridors. We are not a placement agency — we are a workforce management partner who stays engaged throughout the entire employment lifecycle.

From onboarding and attendance management to monthly payroll, ESIC contributions, PF filings, and labour law compliance, every obligation is handled by our experienced operations team. Your management team focuses entirely on production and output targets.

Our worker pool spans all industrial categories, and every candidate goes through document verification, background screening, and a skills assessment before being placed at any client site. With 15,000+ workers currently on active contracts, we have the scale to respond to your requirements quickly — whether you need 5 workers or 500.

Our Process

How It Works

A structured, transparent process from requirement to deployment — with no surprises.

01
Requirement Discussion
We meet with your HR or operations team to understand the number of workers needed, skill categories, shift schedules, site location, and any specific certifications or experience required.
02
Sourcing & Screening
We source candidates from our active worker pool and broader network, conduct document verification, background checks, and skills assessment. Only pre-qualified candidates are shortlisted.
03
Documentation & Onboarding
All employment documents — offer letters, joining forms, PF/ESIC registration, identity verification — are completed before the worker steps on site. No compliance shortcuts.
04
Deployment
Workers are deployed to your facility on the agreed date. Our site coordinator is present for the first day to ensure smooth integration with your team and operations.
05
Ongoing Management
Attendance is tracked, payroll is processed monthly, statutory contributions are filed, and a dedicated account manager remains your single point of contact for all workforce matters.
Key Advantages

Why Clients Choose Kunal Group for Labour Contracting
